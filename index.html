Our Story

About HCIC Vision

A federally incorporated Canadian consultancy with global reach — founded on the belief that true health transformation requires both science and empathy.

Our History

From Vision to Reality

2010 — Founded in Ontario, Canada

HCIC Vision Inc. was federally incorporated with a mission to bridge health coaching with medical innovation.

2012 — First Global Clients

Expanded services internationally, consulting with healthcare organizations across North America and the Middle East.

2016 — Medical Imaging Division

Launched the Health Imaging Consultancy arm, advancing AI integration in diagnostic imaging technologies.

2019 — Mental Health Focus

Added integrative mental health coaching as a dedicated service pillar, led by Dr. Afshin Siddiqui.

Today — Serving the World from Canada

A growing team of certified coaches delivering personalized wellness programs to clients globally.
